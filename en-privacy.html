01What this notice covers

This notice explains where TROPS collects personal information on trops.kr, what we use it for, and when we delete it.

On trops.kr, we collect personal information in one place only: our contact form for inquiries, quote requests, and launch notifications (trops.kr/contact). §02 and §03 below explain what we collect, what we use it for, and when we delete it. You can also email us directly at contact@theo-ne.com; we delete those emails once we have finished responding.

Through August 20, 2026, trops.kr also accepted export pre-check submissions. On September 9, 2026, we deleted all of those submission records and uploaded files, and decommissioned the database that held them. trops.kr no longer holds any pre-check data.

Export pre-check submissions have moved to app.trops.kr. Processing there is covered by that site's own privacy notice.

The “1-minute free check” buttons on this site now lead to app.trops.kr as well (September 21, 2026). What you enter on that screen — including your company name and the product you export — is received by that site and covered by its own privacy notice. trops.kr neither receives nor stores those values.

We do not use advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking tools on this site. We set no advertising cookies, we do not place you in advertising audiences, and we do not share personal information with advertising companies. We do, however, use Google Analytics for visit statistics, and its cookies link multiple visits from the same browser (September 12, 2026; see the third exception below).

The first of three exceptions is a functional cookie, lang, which remembers whether you chose Korean or English (August 21, 2026). Its value is only ever ko or en. It is not used for advertising or analytics, and it is not used to link visits in order to identify you.

The second exception is a temporary visit key (September 4, 2026). To measure which sections you viewed and how long you stayed within a single visit, we need to know which events belong to the same visit. The key is a random value, it disappears when you close the tab (sessionStorage), and it is not connected to any other information we hold. With it, we send a single true/false value showing whether you have visited before. The marker behind that value (trops_seen) only ever holds 1 and is never sent to our servers. As a result, our records alone cannot join two visits to the same person.

The same container holds one more slot: a channel code (trops_vs_f, September 20, 2026). If the address carries ?from=, we keep that value and send it along when you start the 1-minute export readiness check. It tells us which page or partner you came through, and it is not a name, a contact detail or device information. This slot also disappears when you close the tab.

We count how many times each page is opened and how many times the main buttons are clicked (August 18, 2026). Since September 4, 2026, we also measure which sections of a page you viewed, how long you stayed on each, how far down you scrolled, and where you went next. For how you arrived, we keep only one of five categories (search, direct, referral, social, AI) and the domain of the referring site, and we discard the rest of the address.

The answers you choose and the score you see in the 1-minute export readiness check are calculated in your browser only and are not sent to us (September 21, 2026). Between September 20 and 21, 2026 we did send those answers and the score in order to count how many people finished the check; those records contain no name, contact details or company name (the receiving end has no field for them), and their retention and deletion are covered by the privacy notice of app.trops.kr. The 1-minute export readiness check has moved to app.trops.kr, and a check you run there is received by that site.

The measurement we run ourselves, described above, does not record cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, or browser type. Because the temporary key exists only for that visit and disappears when you close the tab, even we cannot tell who visited. We know whether you have been here before only as true or false; we cannot count how many times. The Google Analytics measurement described below is separate, and it does use cookies.

The third exception is Google Analytics 4, a visit-statistics tool (September 12, 2026). We use it to see how often each page is viewed and where visitors leave. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_RQ1NWGK0KM in your browser (for up to two years) and uses them to connect multiple visits from the same browser. Along with them, it sends the address of the page you opened, your device and browser type, your screen size, your approximate location (city level), and how you arrived. Nothing that identifies you personally, such as your name or contact details, is sent. Google uses your IP address to estimate your location and does not store it.

We do not use this data for advertising. The advertising features (Google signals and ad personalization) are turned off, we do not place you in advertising audiences, and we do not share the data with other companies. The data is processed by Google LLC in the United States; §04 lists that cross-border transfer. To opt out, block cookies in your browser settings or install Google's Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Blocking it does not limit your use of this site in any way.

03How long we keep it

Document pre-check submissions. Our rule was to delete the files you uploaded and the submission record 30 days after submission. On September 9, 2026, we deleted everything that remained and decommissioned the database that held it, so we hold nothing under this heading. This is also why our confirmation emails contained a link rather than an attachment: a deletion policy cannot reach an attached file.

If you would rather not wait, email contact@theo-ne.com. We will confirm your request and act on it without delay. Once deleted, the same material cannot be sent to you again.

Inquiries, quote requests, and launch notifications. Inquiries and quote requests are deleted 6 months (183 days) after submission, and launch notifications 1 year (365 days) after submission. An automated cleanup job runs once a day (6:20 a.m. KST) and deletes records that have passed their deadline.

If you would rather not wait, email contact@theo-ne.com. Requests to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information go to the same address, and we act on them without delay once we have confirmed them.

The two emails sent when you submit (the staff notification and your confirmation) are not covered by the automated cleanup above. They stay in our staff mailbox and are deleted once we have finished responding.

Email inquiries. Your message stays only in our staff mailbox. We delete it without delay once we have finished responding, or immediately if you ask sooner.

Records that Korean law requires us to keep (such as contract, payment, and consumer complaint records under the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce) are kept separately for the period that law sets. Payment records are kept for five years, as required by that Act's Enforcement Decree.

Personal information stored as an electronic file is deleted using a technical method that makes recovery or reconstruction impossible. Personal information recorded or stored on paper is destroyed by shredding or incineration.

06How we keep it safe

Storage access keys are used only on the server and are never sent to the browser. Row-level security is enabled on the submission tables, so they cannot be read except through the server.

Confirmation emails contain a link, never an attachment, so that we do not create copies we cannot recall and our deletion policy cannot reach.

Data past its retention period is deleted by a cleanup job.

07When this notice changes

When we change this notice, we post the changes and their effective date on this page. If a change is to your disadvantage, we will tell you before it takes effect.

Effective September 12, 2026: we use a visit-statistics tool

We have added Google Analytics 4 to every page on trops.kr. As a result, two new cookies (_ga and _ga_RQ1NWGK0KM) are set in your browser, and the statistics are transferred to Google LLC in the United States. §01 explains what is sent, and §04 lists the cross-border transfer. We do not use it for advertising. The personal information we collect (§02) and how long we keep it (§03) are unchanged. §01 also explains how to opt out.

Effective September 9, 2026: storage in India (Mumbai) has ended

We deleted all export pre-check data submitted through August 20, 2026, and decommissioned the Supabase project (Mumbai, India region) that held it. We updated the descriptions of remaining data in §01, §02, and §03, and the Mumbai rows in §04, to reflect this. What we collect and how long we keep it are unchanged. The data we held simply no longer exists.

Effective September 8, 2026: storage location changes

The storage location of inquiry, quote request, and launch notification records, and of anonymous page-view and click counts, moves from Supabase's Mumbai, India region to its Seoul, Republic of Korea region. Both tables in §04 have the details.

This amendment takes effect on September 8, 2026, the day it was posted. An earlier notice posted the same day gave an effective date of September 15; the move was completed within the day, so we moved the date up. Because the data is moving from outside Korea (India) into Korea, and what we collect and how long we keep it stay the same, this change is not to your disadvantage.

What we collect and how long we keep it do not change. Neither the items in §02 nor the retention periods (6 months for inquiries and quote requests, 1 year for launch notifications; §02 and §03) change. The processor is the same company (Supabase, Inc.). The only change is the country where the data is stored, and it is moving into Korea.
