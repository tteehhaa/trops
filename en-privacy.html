01What this notice covers

This notice explains where TROPS receives personal information on trops.kr, what we use it for, and when we delete it.

There are two places where we receive personal information — the launch notification sign-up form and the buyer document pre-check intake (/precheck). The tariff lookup (/uae) receives none.

We run no advertising identifiers or behavioural tracking on this site. We set no analytics or advertising cookies, and we keep nothing about you in browser storage.

Lookup records carry no IP address, no browser details and no identifier. We cannot tell who made a lookup.

We never ask for national identification numbers or sensitive categories of data anywhere on this site. Documents you upload may contain such details, so we suggest redacting anything the comparison does not need.

03How long we keep it

Document pre-check intake — thirty days after intake we delete the files you uploaded and the intake record. This is also why the confirmation email carries a link rather than an attachment: a deletion policy cannot reach an attached file.

If you would rather not wait, open the link in your confirmation email and choose to delete your files. We delete them at that moment. Once deleted, the same material cannot be sent to you again.

Launch notification sign-up — there is no database behind this form, so it exists only in our staff mailbox. We delete the message once we have sent the launch notice, or sooner if you ask.

Where Korean law requires records to be kept — contract, payment and consumer complaint records under the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, for example — we keep those for the period that law sets.

04Who processes it for us

05What you can ask us to do

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop us processing it. You may withdraw an optional consent at any time, and doing so does not affect the service you have already requested.

For an intake, the link in your confirmation email shows its status and lets you delete your files immediately. For anything else, write to contact@theo-ne.com and we will handle it.

06How we keep it safe

Storage keys are used on the server only and are never sent to the browser. Intake tables have row level security enabled, so nothing reads them outside the server path.

Confirmation emails carry a link, never an attachment — we avoid creating copies we cannot recall and cannot delete on schedule.

Material past its retention period is removed by a cleanup job.

07When this notice changes

If the content changes we post the change and the date it takes effect on this page. Where a change is to your disadvantage we tell you before it takes effect.

08Not settled yet

The following are for legal review to settle. We have left them blank rather than invent them.

  • Name, title and contact details of the privacy officer (for now, the address above reaches us)
  • Details of any transfer abroad by our processors — countries, timing and method, and who receives the data
  • Routes to remedy, such as the Korean Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee
  • A specific description of our destruction procedure and method
  • The effective date of this notice (set when the final version is issued)

If you need certainty on any of these, write to contact@theo-ne.com. Until the document is settled, what we actually do is exactly what is written above.
